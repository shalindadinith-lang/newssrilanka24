භාරකාර තානාපතිවරයා කැඳවා තිබේ. නිරායුධ සිවිල් වැසියන් පිරිසක් ඉලක්ක කර කිසිදු සාධාරණ හේතුවක් නොමැතිව මාරාන්තික බලය යෙදවීම අනුමත කළ නොහැකි බවත්, එය මූලික මානව හිමිකම් සහ ජාත්‍යන්තර නීතිය බරපතළ ලෙස උල්ලංඝනය කිරීමක් බවත් පාකිස්තාන විදේශ කාර්යාලය නිකුත් කළ නිවේදනයක දැක්වේ. මෙම ඝාතනය සම්බන්ධයෙන් වහාම විනිවිදභාවයෙන් යුත් විශ්වාසනීය පරීක්ෂණයක් පවත්වා, වගකිවයුත්තන්ට එරෙහිව විනය හා නීතිමය පියවර ගෙන ඒ දිස්ත්‍රික්කයේ පදිංචි නිරායුධ සිවිල් වැසියන් තිදෙනෙකු දේශසීමා සලකුණු කණුවෙන් ඔබ්බට පියවර කිහිපයක් තැබූ අවස්ථාවේදී ඉන්දීය දේශසීමා ආරක්ෂකයින් වෙඩි තබා ඇති බවයි. ඉන් දෙදෙනෙකු මියගොස් තෙවැන්නා තුවාල ලබා ඇති අතර, ඔවුන් අනවසරයෙන් දේශසීමා ආක්‍රමණය කළ පිරිසක් නොවන බව පාකිස්තාන බලධාරීහු තරයේ ප්‍රකාශ කරති. කෙසේවෙතත්, ඉන්දීය ආරක්ෂක අංශ මේ සම්බන්ධයෙන් පරස්පර ප්‍රකාශයක් සිදුකර ඇති බව ඉන්දීය මාධ්‍ය වාර්තා කරයි. ඉන්දීය පන්ජාබ් ප්‍රාන්තයේ ටාර්න් ටාරන් දිස්ත්‍රික්කයට අයත් ජාත්‍යන්තර දේශසීමාව ඔස්සේ සැකකටයුතු පුද්ගලයන් තිදෙනෙකු ඉන්දීය භූමියට ඇතුළු වීමට උත්සාහ කළ බව ටාර්න් ටාරන් නියෝජ්‍ය පොලිස් අධිකාරී අතුල් තැබීම සිදු කළ බවත්, මියගිය දෙදෙනාගේ අනන්‍යතාව තහවුරු කරගැනීමට පරීක්ෂණ පවත්වන බවත් ඉන්දීය පාර්ශ්වය සඳහන් කරයි. කාශ්මීර් දේශසීමාවේ නිරන්තර වෙඩි හුවමාරු සිදු වුවද, කිලෝමීටර් 553ක් දිගැති පන්ජාබ් දේශසීමා තීරය සාමාන්‍යයෙන් සාමකාමී කලාපයක් ලෙස පැවතිණි. 2025 අප්‍රේල් මාසයේදී ඉන්දීය පාලන ප්‍රදේශයේ සංචාරකයින් 26 දෙනෙකු ඝාතනය වීමේ සිදුවීම මුල් කරගනිමින් දෙරට අතර ඩ්‍රෝන සහ මිසයිල ප්‍රහාර හුවමාරු වෙමින් දරුණු විසින් ප්‍රතික්ෂේප කර පැය විසිහතරක් ගතවීමටත් පෙර මෙම සිදුවීම වාර්තා වීම දෙරටේ සබඳතා තවදුරටත් අවිනිශ්චිත තත්ත්වයකට ඇද දමා ඇත. Escalating Tensions Between India and Pakistan Following Fatal Shooting Incident Two Pakistani nationals lost their lives due to a shooting incident carried out by the Indian Border Security Force in the Punjab border region, heightening already strained relations between the two countries, as reported by 'The Hindu' and the Associated Press. In response to the incident, Pakistan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs summoned the Indian Deputy High Commissioner in Islamabad to express its strong condemnation. The ministry emphasized that targeting unarmed civilians with lethal force is unacceptable and constitutes a stark violation of fundamental human rights and international law, as detailed in an official statement. Pakistan has called upon India to conduct a transparent and credible investigation into the killings and to inform Islamabad of the disciplinary and legal actions taken against those responsible. According to Pakistan Television, security forces claimed that three unarmed civilians from the Kasur district of eastern Pakistan crossed the international border when they were shot by Indian border guards. While two of the civilians were reported dead, the third sustained injuries; Pakistani officials assert that they were not part of any unauthorized cross-border operation. On the other hand, Indian media reports indicated that Indian security forces made a contrasting statement. The Deputy Inspector General of Police for Tarn Taran district disclosed that three suspects attempted to infiltrate Indian territory through the international border. While one suspect managed to retreat back to Pakistan, the other two, disregarding warnings from security forces, attempted to cross into Indian territory, resulting in the shooting incident. The Indian side is currently conducting an investigation to confirm the identities of the deceased. Despite ongoing sporadic exchanges of fire along the Kashmir frontier, the Punjab border, stretching 553 kilometers, has typically been seen as a peaceful region. The recent incident follows a tragic event in April 2025, when 26 Indian tourists were killed in the Indian-administered territory, marking a significant escalation that has led to a surge in tensions with drone and missile strikes exchanged between the two nations in the interim. The situation has further deteriorated following reports of the assassination of a senior official from the Lashkar-e-Taiba organization, with claims from India that the Pakistani military has denied. Escalating Tensions Between India and Pakistan Following Fatal Shooting Incident

Two Pakistani nationals lost their lives due to a shooting incident carried out by the Indian Border Security Force in the Punjab border region, heightening already strained relations between the two countries, as reported by 'The Hindu' and the Associated Press.

In response to the incident, Pakistan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs summoned the Indian Deputy High Commissioner in Islamabad to express its strong condemnation.

The ministry emphasized that targeting unarmed civilians with lethal force is unacceptable and constitutes a stark violation of fundamental human rights and international law, as detailed in an official statement.

Pakistan has called upon India to conduct a transparent and credible investigation into the killings and to inform Islamabad of the disciplinary and legal actions taken against those responsible.

According to Pakistan Television, security forces claimed that three unarmed civilians from the Kasur district of eastern Pakistan crossed the international border when they were shot by Indian border guards. While two of the civilians were reported dead, the third sustained injuries; Pakistani officials assert that they were not part of any unauthorized cross-border operation.

On the other hand, Indian media reports indicated that Indian security forces made a contrasting statement. The Deputy Inspector General of Police for Tarn Taran district disclosed that three suspects attempted to infiltrate Indian territory through the international border.

While one suspect managed to retreat back to Pakistan, the other two, disregarding warnings from security forces, attempted to cross into Indian territory, resulting in the shooting incident. The Indian side is currently conducting an investigation to confirm the identities of the deceased.

Despite ongoing sporadic exchanges of fire along the Kashmir frontier, the Punjab border, stretching 553 kilometers, has typically been seen as a peaceful region. The recent incident follows a tragic event in April 2025, when 26 Indian tourists were killed in the Indian-administered territory, marking a significant escalation that has led to a surge in tensions with drone and missile strikes exchanged between the two nations in the interim. The situation has further deteriorated following reports of the assassination of a senior official from the Lashkar-e-Taiba organization, with claims from India that the Pakistani military has denied. This latest shooting incident has plunged Indo-Pak relations into an even more uncertain state.
