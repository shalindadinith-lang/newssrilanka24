Shortly after 6:30 a.m. yesterday, the driver arrived at the corporation’s headquarters located on Stafford Street in Colombo 06. Furthermore, the driver’s phone was unreachable, prompting the transportation manager of the corporation to file a complaint with the Vallawatta Police. Police have identified the missing 29-year-old driver as a resident of the Ragama, Kandaliyeddapaluwa area. As the vehicle failed to arrive at the expected time, the Chairman made inquiries via phone, during which the driver mentioned there was an issue with the fuel gauge. Consequently, the Chairman directed him to a vehicle repair facility in the Kadawatha area for inspection, where technicians reportedly confirmed that there were no faults with the vehicle.

However, the driver did not arrive at the office by 4:00 p.m. that day. Furthermore, the driver’s phone was unreachable, prompting the transportation manager of the corporation to file a complaint with the Vallawatta Police.

Police have identified the missing 29-year-old driver as a resident of the Ragama, Kandaliyeddapaluwa area. Investigators are currently conducting a thorough probe, utilizing phone analysis reports and CCTV footage to trace his whereabouts.
