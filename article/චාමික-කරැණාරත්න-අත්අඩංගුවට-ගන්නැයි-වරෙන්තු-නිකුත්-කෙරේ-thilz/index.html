Court Issues Warrant for Sri Lankan Cricketer Chamika Karunaratne Over Child Support Case

In a significant development, the Colombo Additional Magistrate, Kasun Kanchana Dasanayaka, has issued a warrant for the arrest of Sri Lankan cricketer Chamika Karunaratne. This action comes in response to Karunaratne's failure to appear in court regarding a child support case filed by flight attendant Rasika Sewwandi.

The magistrate's decision was prompted by Karunaratne's absence during the proceedings, which led to the issuance of the warrant. During the hearing, the court was informed that Sewwandi had initiated legal action to establish paternity in order to secure maintenance funds for her child, for whom Karunaratne has been named as the father.

Sewwandi had previously petitioned the General Registrar to issue a birth certificate that includes Karunaratne's name and sought court authorization for a DNA test to confirm paternity. On August 4, she sought a ruling from the court to obtain blood samples from the cricketer for the necessary testing.

However, when the Colombo District Judge, Lalith Kannanagara, reviewed the request on the same day, he noted that the complainant had failed to present sufficient evidence to justify obtaining blood samples for the DNA test, resulting in the dismissal of the application.
