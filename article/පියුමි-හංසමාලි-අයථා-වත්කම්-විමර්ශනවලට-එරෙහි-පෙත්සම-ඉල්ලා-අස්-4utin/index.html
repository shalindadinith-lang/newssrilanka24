Model Piyumi Hansamali Withdraws Legal Challenge Against Ongoing Investigations In a significant development, model Piyumi Hansamali has decided to withdraw her legal challenge aimed at questioning the legitimacy of ongoing investigations conducted by the Criminal Investigation Department (CID) into questionable asset acquisition. This move comes amidst her ongoing legal predicament. Representing the Attorney General, Additional Solicitor General Shani Kularatne informed the court that the CID is indeed involved in an extensive investigation regarding Hansamali's assets. He noted, however, that it is impossible to provide a specific timeline for the conclusion of these inquiries. Kularatne further emphasized that the CID requires additional time to thoroughly complete the investigation. The appeal was heard today before a two-judge bench of the Court of Appeal, comprising Justices Mayadunne Koraya and Lakmali Karunanayake. During the proceedings, Ronald Pereira, appearing on behalf of Piyumi Hansamali, argued that since his client has not been arrested to date, she should be granted permission to withdraw her application. He also requested that should the need arise in the future, Hansamali retains the right to file a new application. Model Piyumi Hansamali Withdraws Legal Challenge Against Ongoing Investigations

In a significant development, model Piyumi Hansamali has decided to withdraw her legal challenge aimed at questioning the legitimacy of ongoing investigations conducted by the Criminal Investigation Department (CID) into questionable asset acquisition. This move comes amidst her ongoing legal predicament.

Representing the Attorney General, Additional Solicitor General Shani Kularatne informed the court that the CID is indeed involved in an extensive investigation regarding Hansamali's assets. He noted, however, that it is impossible to provide a specific timeline for the conclusion of these inquiries.

Kularatne further emphasized that the CID requires additional time to thoroughly complete the investigation.

The appeal was heard today before a two-judge bench of the Court of Appeal, comprising Justices Mayadunne Koraya and Lakmali Karunanayake.

During the proceedings, Ronald Pereira, appearing on behalf of Piyumi Hansamali, argued that since his client has not been arrested to date, she should be granted permission to withdraw her application. He also requested that should the need arise in the future, Hansamali retains the right to file a new application.

After considering the arguments presented, the Court of Appeal duly granted Hansamali permission to withdraw her application.
