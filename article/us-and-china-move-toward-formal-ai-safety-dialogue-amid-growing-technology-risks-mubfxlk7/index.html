US and China Move Toward Formal AI Safety Dialogue Amid Growing Technology Risks

News Sri Lanka 24    2026 බිනර 21

The United States and China are moving toward establishing a formal dialogue on artificial intelligence safety, as concerns grow over the risks posed by increasingly powerful and autonomous AI systems.

The discussions took place between senior officials from Washington and Beijing, with the two sides considering a communication mechanism that could be used when serious AI-related incidents occur. According to U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, the proposed framework would include an “incident line” through which the two countries could communicate about significant AI safety events. The discussions are intended to improve transparency and reduce the risk of misunderstandings between the world's two leading AI powers. ## Focus on AI-related security incidents One of the main areas under discussion is how governments should respond if advanced AI systems behave unexpectedly or become involved in potentially dangerous activities. Officials are expected to discuss issues including autonomous AI agents, cyberattacks involving non-state actors and other scenarios that could create national-security concerns. The proposal comes as AI systems are becoming increasingly capable of performing tasks with limited human intervention. This has raised questions about how governments can respond quickly when an AI-related incident crosses national borders. Bessent said the two sides want to develop protocols that would help establish a common understanding of major AI dangers and provide a channel for communication when such incidents occur. ## Next meeting planned in Shenzhen The United States and China are expected to continue the AI safety discussions in approximately two months, with the next meeting planned for **Shenzhen, China**. The upcoming discussions could provide an opportunity for officials to explore more detailed rules for communication and incident reporting. However, the two countries remain major competitors in artificial intelligence, particularly in advanced AI models, semiconductors and computing technology. This means that cooperation on safety issues is taking place alongside broader technological competition. ## AI safety becomes a global concern The latest discussions reflect a wider international debate over how increasingly autonomous AI systems should be managed. Experts from the United States and China have also previously discussed safeguards around AI systems that could affect military operations, critical infrastructure or cyber systems. Some proposals have called for maintaining meaningful human control over highly consequential AI decisions and establishing direct communication channels for unexpected incidents. Neither side has indicated that the latest discussions will end their technological competition. Instead, the proposed dialogue appears focused on creating a way for Washington and Beijing to communicate when advanced AI systems create serious safety or security concerns. As AI capabilities continue to develop rapidly, the outcome of these discussions could become an important part of the broader international effort to establish common approaches to AI safety and responsible development. **Source:** Reuters reporting and related public statements from U.S.-China officials.
