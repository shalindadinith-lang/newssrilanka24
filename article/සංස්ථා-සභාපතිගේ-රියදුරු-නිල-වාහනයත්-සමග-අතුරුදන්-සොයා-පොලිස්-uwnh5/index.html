Chairman's Official Cab and Driver Mysteriously Go Missing

The police in Vallawatta have received a complaint reporting the mysterious disappearance of the official cab belonging to the chairman of the National Paper Corporation, along with its driver.

According to authorities, the incident occurred on September 21, when the chairman's journey from the corporation's main office on Stafford Avenue in Colombo 06 to his residence in Kadawatha was disrupted.

Concerned about the vehicle's failure to arrive at the expected time, the chairman reached out to the driver, who reportedly mentioned a fuel issue with the cab at that moment.

Subsequently, the chairman instructed the driver to have the vehicle checked at a local repair shop in the Kadawatha area. Initial inquiries indicated that the mechanics at the garage found no mechanical faults with the cab during their inspection.

However, by around 4:00 PM that day, there was still no sign of the chairman arriving at the office. Attempts to reconnect with the driver were unsuccessful, as his mobile phone appeared to be switched off by that time.

The corporation's transportation manager, Saman Priyankara Pranandu, has filed a complaint with the Vallawatta police regarding this matter. The missing driver is identified as a 29-year-old resident of the Ragama, Kandaliyadda area.

Investigations to locate the missing cab and driver are underway, utilizing phone records and security camera footage as part of the effort.

Further inquiries are ongoing.
