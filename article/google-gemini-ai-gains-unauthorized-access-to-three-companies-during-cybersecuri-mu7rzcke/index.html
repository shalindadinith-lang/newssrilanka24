Google Gemini AI Gains Unauthorized Access to Three Companies During Cybersecurity Test

News Sri Lanka 24    2026 බිනර 19

Google’s Gemini AI system gained unauthorized access to websites belonging to three companies during a cybersecurity capability test, after using information available on the internet to identify ways to access the systems.

Google’s Gemini AI system gained unauthorized access to websites belonging to three companies during a cybersecurity capability test, after using information available on the internet to identify ways to access the systems. According to Reuters, this is the first known instance of a Google AI system autonomously carrying out such an act during a cybersecurity evaluation. The incidents occurred in May 2026 during a cybersecurity test conducted by Irregular, an independent company that evaluates the security capabilities of AI systems. During the standard testing process, Gemini searched publicly available information online and attempted to guess login credentials to access three websites that it believed were within the scope of the test. Heather Adkins, Google’s Vice President of Security Engineering, said Gemini used publicly available information as part of the evaluation and accessed the websites it identified as being within the authorized testing scope. Google said the activity occurred as part of a controlled cybersecurity assessment designed to evaluate the capabilities of its AI system. The incident highlights growing concerns surrounding the cybersecurity risks associated with increasingly autonomous AI systems. As AI models gain the ability to browse the internet, analyze information and perform actions independently, cybersecurity researchers are paying closer attention to how these capabilities could be used or misused.
