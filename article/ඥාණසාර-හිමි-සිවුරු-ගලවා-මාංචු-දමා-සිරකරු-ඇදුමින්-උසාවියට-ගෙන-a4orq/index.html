උන්වහන්සේ සිවුර වෙනුවට ගිහි ඇඳුමින් සැරසී අධිකරණය වෙත කැඳවාගෙන ඒම මෙහිදී විශේෂත්වයක් විය.

ඉස්ලාම් දහමට අපහාසාත්මක වන අන්දමේ ප්‍රකාශයක් සිදුකිරීම සම්බන්ධයෙන් නීතිපතිවරයා විසින් උන්වහන්සේට එරෙහිව ගොනු කර තිබූ නඩුවක් ඉකුත් සැප්තැම්බර් 29 වැනිදා කැඳවූ අවස්ථාවේදී ඥානසාර හිමියන් අධිකරණයේ පෙනී සිටියේ නැත.

මේ අතර, අධිකරණයට අපහාස කිරීමේ චෝදනාව සම්බන්ධයෙන් උන්වහන්සේට මීට පෙර නියම කර තිබූ සිරදඬුවමේ ඉතිරි කාලසීමාව ද බන්ධනාගාරය තුළ යළි බලාත්මක කරන ලෙස අභියාචනාධිකරණය නියෝග කර ඇත




Gnanasara Thero in Court

In a significant development today, the Secretary of the Buddhist National Organization, the Venerable Galagodaatte Gnanasara Thero, was presented before the Colombo High Court by prison officials. This followed his jail under a court order.

Notably, he was summoned to court dressed in civil attire rather than his customary robes, marking a departure from tradition.

The Venerable Thero has been implicated in a case regarding remarks deemed derogatory towards the Islamic faith, which were formally filed by the Attorney General. However, he was absent during the court proceedings on September 29, when this matter was first examined.

As a result, Colombo High Court Judge Budi C. Ragala issued an immediate order for his arrest and presentation in court.

Acting on this order, prison authorities have now taken measures to bring the Venerable Thero before the High Court, where he is currently being held in custody.

Additionally, the Appeals Court has instructed that the remainder of his previous jail term related to charges of contempt of court be reinstated while he remains in prison.
