MediaTek Unveils New Smartphone Chip with 2nm Technology

News Sri Lanka 24    2026 බිනර 15

MediaTek has introduced its new Dimensity 9600 Pro smartphone chipset, attracting significant attention in the technology world. According to MediaTek's announcement on September 15, the Dimensity 9600 Pro is the company's first smartphone chipset manufactured using TSMC's advanced 2-nanometre (2nm) manufacturing process.

Alongside the new chip, MediaTek has also introduced another chipset called the Dimensity 9600M. It uses TSMC's 3nm process and is designed for the flagship smartphone segment targeting a wider range of users. 🚀 Why Is 2nm Technology Important? Moving a chip's manufacturing process down to the 2nm level makes it possible to integrate a greater number of transistors into a smaller area. This creates new opportunities for future smartphones to deliver higher processing performance, improved power efficiency, and stronger AI processing capabilities. However, actual improvements in battery life and performance will also depend on the phone's overall hardware and software design. 📱 When Will It Reach Smartphones? According to MediaTek, the first smartphones powered by the Dimensity 9600 Pro and Dimensity 9600M are expected to arrive in the market soon. This represents another important step in the competition among smartphone processor manufacturers. Advanced chips such as these are expected to play an increasingly important role in areas such as AI, gaming, camera processing, and energy efficiency in future smartphones.
